**Written description of the interaction model, including the objective of the interaction**

The model that I'm using for my game comes in two parts, the shop and the in-game model. In the shop, players can hover over effects and buy them to put on their Tetris pieces, or buy new pieces.
They can also refresh the shop, which costs increasingly more money. When ready, they can click continue to move onto fighting an enemy.

When fighting an enemy, players will play Tetris with the pieces they've made. By clearing lines players can damage enemies, as well as use the effects they've added to the pieces.
Enemies will attack players in many different ways, the main one being putting negative effects on the background of the game board. If a player places a piece on that cell, they'll
get hit by it.

**At least two examples of the interaction implemented**
Right now I have this implemented by being able to move tetris pieces and clear lines, after you place one piece another will come, until you die or win the fight. I also have
the shop working, which will pop up after every combat.


**Brief write-up on how this interaction model can be used in different parts of the game and why**
The combat interaction model can be used throughout the whole game. While different enemies might have different attacks and special abilities, the core interaction model will
always stay the same. Since my game is a roguelike, you progress through enemies and eventually reach a boss. I haven't fully decided how the structure of a run will look yet
though. The shop also will be able to be interacted with at multiple points in the game, either after every combat, or after reaching certain areas etc.
